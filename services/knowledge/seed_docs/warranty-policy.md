# Warranty Policy

Our warranty protects you if a product stops working because of a manufacturing defect. This page explains how long the warranty lasts, what it covers, and how to make a claim.

## Warranty period by product type

The warranty starts on the **delivery date** of your order:

- **Electronics: 12-month warranty.** This includes smartwatches, headphones, earbuds, speakers, phones, desk lamps and other electronic devices.
- **Accessories: 6-month warranty.** This includes phone cases, bags, backpacks and similar accessories.
- **Clothing: no warranty.** Clothing, shoes and socks are not covered by a warranty, but they can be returned within the return window if they are unused.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers **manufacturing defects**: problems caused by how the product was made, not by how it was used. For example, it covers a smartwatch that will not turn on or charge, headphones with a faulty speaker, or a zip on a backpack that breaks under normal use.

If a covered defect is confirmed, we will repair or replace the product. If a repair or replacement is not possible, we will offer a refund.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty **does not cover**:

- **Physical damage**, such as drops, cracks, dents or a broken screen.
- **Water damage**, such as liquid spills or using a product in water when it is not waterproof.
- Normal wear and tear, scratches, or damage from repairs done by someone else.
- Lost or stolen products.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Open a support ticket and use the chat.
2. Tell us your **order number** and which product has the problem.
3. Describe the problem, and share photos if you can.

Our support team will check the claim and explain the next steps.

## Warranty and returns are separate

The warranty is **separate from the return window**. The return window (30 days from delivery, or 15 days for electronics) is for returning a product you do not want. The warranty is for a product that develops a defect later. For example, if your smartwatch stops working after two months, the return window has passed, but it is still covered by the 12-month electronics warranty.

## Quick answers

- **What does the warranty cover on a smartwatch?** A smartwatch is electronics, so it has a 12-month warranty from delivery covering manufacturing defects, but not physical or water damage.
- **Is clothing covered by a warranty?** No.
- **How long is the warranty on accessories?** 6 months from delivery.
