# Shipping Policy

This page explains how long delivery takes, what shipping costs, how to track your order, and what to do if a delivery is late.

## Delivery times

We offer two delivery options:

- **Standard delivery: 3 to 7 business days.** Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days from the day your order ships.
- **Express delivery: 1 to 3 business days.** Express delivery takes 1 to 3 business days from the day your order ships.

Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays. Each order shows an estimated delivery date once it has shipped.

## Shipping costs

**Shipping is free on orders above ₹999.** For orders of ₹999 or less, a shipping fee is shown at checkout before you pay. Express delivery may have an extra charge, which is also shown at checkout.

## Where we deliver

We currently deliver **within India only**. We are not able to ship orders to addresses outside India.

## How to track your order

Once your order has shipped, you can track it:

1. Open your order in your account.
2. Look for the **carrier** (the delivery company, for example BlueDart or Delhivery) and the **tracking number** shown on the order.
3. Use the tracking number on the carrier's website to see where your package is.

Order statuses also tell you where your order is: placed (being prepared), shipped (on its way) and delivered. If your order still says placed, it has not left our warehouse yet, so there is no tracking number.

You can also ask our support team in a ticket chat, and we will share the latest tracking details with you.

## If your order is late

Sometimes deliveries take a little longer than expected because of weather, holidays or high demand. Please allow a few extra days.

If your order is **more than 10 days past its estimated delivery date**, please open a support ticket and tell us your order number. We will contact the carrier, find out what happened, and help you with next steps, such as a replacement or a refund.

## Quick answers

- **How long does standard delivery take?** 3 to 7 business days.
- **How long does express delivery take?** 1 to 3 business days.
- **Is shipping free?** Yes, for orders above ₹999.
- **How can I track my order?** Use the carrier and tracking number shown on your order.
- **Do you ship internationally?** No, we deliver within India only.
