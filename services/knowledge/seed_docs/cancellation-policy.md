# Cancellation Policy

Changed your mind? This page explains when you can cancel an order, what happens after an order has shipped, and how cancellation refunds work.

## When you can cancel an order

You can cancel an order **only while its status is placed**. Placed means the order has been received and is being prepared, but **has not shipped yet**.

To cancel, open a support ticket, tell us your order number, and ask us to cancel it. If the order has not shipped, we will cancel it for you.

Once an order is cancelled, its status changes to **cancelled**.

## Can I cancel an order after it has shipped?

**No. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled.** After shipping, the package is already with the delivery company and on its way to you.

But you still have an option: **after the order is delivered, you can return the items** under our Returns Policy. Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery (15 days for electronics). Gift cards and perishable items cannot be returned.

So, if your order has already shipped:

1. Wait for the order to be delivered.
2. Open a support ticket with your order number and the item you want to return.
3. We will check the item is eligible and help you with the return and refund.

If you do not want the package, please do not refuse it without contacting us first; we can guide you through the simplest option.

## Refunds for cancelled orders

If you paid in advance, **cancelled orders are refunded within 5 to 7 business days**. The money goes back to your original payment method (card, UPI or net banking).

If you chose cash on delivery, there is nothing to refund, because you have not paid yet.

## Quick answers

- **Can I cancel my order?** Yes, if it is still placed and has not shipped.
- **Can I cancel an order after it has shipped?** No. You can return the items after delivery instead.
- **How long does a cancellation refund take?** 5 to 7 business days, to your original payment method.
- **Can I cancel just one item?** Please contact support with your order number and we will tell you what is possible for your order.
