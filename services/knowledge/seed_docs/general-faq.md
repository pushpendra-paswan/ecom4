# General FAQ

Answers to common questions about contacting support, order statuses and paying for your order.

## How to contact support

The best way to reach us is to **open a support ticket and chat with us**:

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Open a new support ticket with a short subject, such as "Return for ORD-1234".
3. Describe your question in the chat. Include your order number if your question is about an order.

All messages about your question stay together in the same ticket, so you never have to repeat yourself.

## Support hours

- **Our AI assistant is available 24/7.** It can answer questions about our policies, look up your orders and help you start a return at any time.
- **Our human support agents are available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 19:00 IST.** If you ask to speak to a person outside these hours, an agent will pick up your ticket when support hours begin.

## What do the order statuses mean?

Every order has one of these statuses:

- **Placed:** we have received your order and are preparing it. It has not shipped yet, and you can still cancel it.
- **Shipped:** your order has left our warehouse and is on its way. You can track it with the carrier and tracking number shown on the order. It can no longer be cancelled.
- **Delivered:** your order has arrived. From the delivery date, the return window and the warranty begin.
- **Cancelled:** the order was cancelled before it shipped. If you paid in advance, the money is refunded to your original payment method.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept these payment methods:

- **Credit cards**
- **Debit cards**
- **UPI**
- **Net banking**
- **Cash on delivery**

Refunds always go back to the original payment method you used.

## Do I need an account to order?

Yes. An account lets you see your orders, track deliveries and open support tickets.

## Where can I find your policies?

You can ask us about any of our policies in the support chat:

- **Returns Policy:** what can be returned and how many days you have.
- **Refund Policy:** how much is refunded and how long it takes.
- **Shipping Policy:** delivery times, shipping costs and tracking.
- **Cancellation Policy:** when an order can be cancelled.
- **Warranty Policy:** what is covered if a product is defective.

## Quick answers

- **When is support available?** The AI assistant 24/7; human agents Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 19:00 IST.
- **What payment methods do you accept?** Credit and debit cards, UPI, net banking and cash on delivery.
- **How do I contact support?** Open a ticket and chat with us.
