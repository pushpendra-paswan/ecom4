# Refund Policy

This page explains how much you get back when you return an item, how refunds are approved, and how long it takes for the money to reach your account.

## How much is refunded

The refund amount is **the price you paid for the returned item**. If you return one item from an order with several items, only that item's price is refunded.

**Original shipping fees are not refunded.**

## Refund approval: small and large refunds

Every refund goes through a quick check before it is processed:

- **Refunds of ₹5,000 or less are processed automatically.** As soon as your return is accepted, the refund is approved and processed straight away.
- **Refunds above ₹5,000 are reviewed by our support team** before they are processed. Large refunds need this approval step. The review usually takes **within one business day**, and we will update you in your support ticket once it is done.

## How long a refund takes to reach your account

After the refund has been processed, the money goes back to your **original payment method** (the card, UPI account or bank account you paid with). It usually takes **5 to 7 business days** for the refund to reach your account, depending on your bank.

If you paid with cash on delivery, our support team will contact you in the ticket chat to arrange the refund.

Timeline at a glance:

1. You request a return in the support chat.
2. The return is accepted.
3. The refund is approved (automatically for ₹5,000 or less, after review for larger amounts).
4. The refund is processed.
5. The money reaches your original payment method in 5 to 7 business days.

## If a refund is rejected

Sometimes a refund cannot be approved, for example when the item does not meet the conditions in our Returns Policy. If your refund is rejected, **we will always tell you the reason** in your support ticket, so you know exactly what happened and what your options are.

## Quick answers

- **How long does a refund take to reach my account?** 5 to 7 business days after the refund is processed.
- **Do large refunds need approval?** Yes. Refunds above ₹5,000 are reviewed by our support team first, usually within one business day. Refunds of ₹5,000 or less are processed automatically.
- **Will I get my shipping fee back?** No, original shipping fees are not refunded.
- **Where does my refund go?** Back to the original payment method you used.
